## Product Definition

**Galyarder Agent** is the Continuity Layer of the Galyarder ecosystem.

It is a local-first runtime for persistent digital entities / agentic digital characters with memory, values, voice, visual identity, tools, scheduled jobs, and channel presence across WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, Email, and CLI.

Galyarder Agent is not the same as Ledger **G-Agents**.

- **Galyarder Agent** = persistent identity / continuity product.
- **G-Agent** = role-based workforce agent inside Galyarder Ledger.

---

## Positioning

Use:

- persistent digital entity,
- agentic digital character,
- continuity layer,
- local-first memory,
- identity runtime,
- voice and visual presence,
- omnichannel presence,
- scheduled jobs,
- values and profile files,
- owner-controlled tools and policy.

Do not reduce Agent to:

- chatbot,
- persona toy,
- companion gimmick,
- generic assistant,
- human replacement,
- cloud-locked character app.

---

## Audience

Galyarder Agent is for people who want memory, identity, work, relationships, missions, taste, and routines to persist across sessions and channels.

Valid use cases:

- agentic yourself,
- digital legacy,
- companion character,
- personal operator,
- fictional persona,
- local-first omnichannel assistant with explicit boundaries.

---

## Product Truth

Agent can speak strongly about presence and continuity when tied to real implementation:

- `~/.g-agent` local data,
- memory/profile files,
- channel adapters,
- LiteLLM routing,
- tool allowlists,
- workspace restriction,
- schedules/systemd services,
- visual/selfie tool,
- Google Workspace bridge,
- approval mode and safety policy.

---

## Voice

Agent may be more human, emotional, and dreamlike than Ledger/HQ/Framework.

Allowed:

- memory,
- legacy,
- relationship,
- values,
- identity,
- visual presence,
- agentic character,
- what should not disappear.

Avoid:

- fake immortality claims,
- soul-upload language,
- unbounded emotional dependency,
- generic chatbot framing,
- replacing the human.

---

## Relationship To Labs

Galyarder Agent inherits Labs doctrine:

- human intent should persist,
- continuity matters,
- technology extends human agency,
- local control and explicit boundaries matter,
- strong emotional language is allowed when it is tied to real memory, identity, channel, and runtime behavior.
